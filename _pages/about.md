I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at the [University of Notre Dame](https://www.nd.edu/), advised by Dr. Zhi Zheng in the [Intelligent Interaction Research (I²R) Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/iirl/home). I am also an affiliated researcher at the [Indiana University School of Medicine](https://medicine.iu.edu/).

My research focuses on **trustworthy and human-centered AI for healthcare**, with an emphasis on developing intelligent conversational agents that provide reliable, personalized, and context-aware support in high-stakes care settings. Currently, I am building agentic conversational AI systems for dementia caregiver support, and benchmarks for evaluating large language models on Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementias (ADRD).

Before Notre Dame, I received an M.Sc. in Communications and Signal Processing (Distinction) from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/). I received a B.Eng. in Electronic and Electrical Engineering (First Class Honours) from the [University of Birmingham](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/) and a B.Eng. in Photoelectric Science and Engineering from the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/).
